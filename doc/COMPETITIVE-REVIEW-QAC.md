# ENCAP competitive review: QAC Group
**Review date:** 22 October 2026  
**Reference:** https://qac-group.com/

## What QAC presents
- Company purpose, mission, vision and values.
- A “why choose us” message and customer-testimonial section.
- Product catalog pages that can show varieties, harvest windows, size/grade information and packaging.
- A detailed packing-house page with supply-chain, cold-chain, traceability and accreditation messaging.
- Contact locations, a map embed, social profiles and working-hour information.

## Existing ENCAP strengths
ENCAP already has bilingual Blade pages for About, farms, packing stations, quality, export/logistics, products, category detail, gallery, contact and quote enquiries. It has a category CMS, category images/gallery, available formats, packaging fields, central SEO/site settings, contact and WhatsApp fields, page metadata, canonical and language alternates, sitemap.xml and robots.txt. Its copy explicitly avoids claiming a certification, specification or capability until confirmed.

## Implemented from this review
1. A bilingual mission/vision/values section on About.
2. Optional, category-managed varieties, harvest window and specifications/size/grade fields. They are shown on public product pages only when an administrator fills them.
3. Optional business-hours and official social-profile fields in the central site settings.
4. A map-search link on Contact when an address is configured.
5. A correction so the Packing Stations page accepts central SEO overrides just like the other public pages.

## Not copied without evidence
- Customer testimonials: no approved named or anonymized customer quotes were provided.
- Export counts, destination counts, “100% organic” or similar numerical/quality claims.
- Certifications, memberships and downloadable accreditation documents.
- QAC's business address, business hours, imagery, logos or company-specific joint-venture details.

Before publishing product data, certifications, testimonials or metrics, ENCAP must supply the approved factual details. Generic or stock imagery must not be represented as a company-owned farm or facility.
